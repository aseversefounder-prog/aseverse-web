# AseVerse Product Brief

**App name:** AseVerse  
**Tagline:** "Ink the words. Amplify the voices."  
**Domain:** aseverse.com

## Core Idea

AseVerse is a creator-first storytelling platform for authors, readers, and voice actors. It supports novels, history, science, educational stories, audiobooks, book clubs, and community discussion.

The product should feel like a mix of Wattpad, Audible, a creator dashboard, and a community space, with stronger tools for creators and voice actors.

## Main User Types

### Readers

Readers can browse stories and audiobooks, read chapters, listen to narration, switch between reading and listening mid-chapter, join book clubs, highlight and comment inside chapters, save books to TBR, resume progress, customize reading themes and typography, download offline content, and earn coins or points through reading, check-ins, ads, or engagement.

### Authors / Creators

Creators can upload stories, add story details, upload cover art, choose text/audio/both formats, monetize stories, view analytics, invite voice actors, manage chapters, offer exclusive content, and unlock badges or creator tiers.

### Voice Actors / Narrators

Voice actors can create profiles, upload voice samples, audition or collaborate on stories, receive author invites, monetize narration work, be credited on story pages, and potentially replace AI narration when selected.

## Core App Sections

### Landing Page / Public Website

Include a hero section explaining AseVerse, waitlist signup, "Join the Waitlist" and "Become a Creator" buttons, a short platform description, feature highlights, a founder/coming soon message, and the aseverse.com domain.

Feature highlights:

- Read and listen in one place
- Creator monetization
- Voice actor collaboration
- Book clubs and community
- Stories beyond novels: history, science, and educational content

### Login / Sign Up

Basic flow:

- Email
- Password
- Continue button
- Optional Google sign-up later
- Account type selection: Reader, Creator, Voice Actor, or Both Creator + Reader

### Home / Discover Page

Include top navigation/header, profile avatar, search, featured story, "For You", "Continue Reading", "New Audiobooks", "Trending in AseVerse", "Book Clubs", and horizontal story cards.

Story cards should show cover image, title, author, genre, rating or likes, audio/text badge, and progress if started.

### Story Detail Page

Include cover image, title, author, narrator when available, genre/tags, description, chapter list, primary action buttons, status, and monetization label.

Buttons:

- Read
- Listen
- Save to TBR
- Join Discussion
- Download Offline

Statuses and monetization:

- Ongoing / Complete
- Free
- Ad unlock
- Premium
- Coin unlock

### Reading Page

Include chapter title, body text, highlight/comment/bookmark features, font controls, font size controls, dark/white/warm themes, audio play button while reading, mid-chapter audio switching, and progress indicator.

### Audio Player

Include cover image, story title, chapter title, narrator name, play/pause, skip forward/back, speed control, sleep timer, progress bar, download, ambient music toggle, separate ambient volume, future CarPlay support, and cross-device resume.

### Dual Experience Mode

Dual Experience is a signature feature. It lets users read and listen at the same time, follow along while audio plays, switch between reading and listening without losing place, use split-screen on tablet/web, and stacked text/audio controls on mobile.

### Book Clubs / Discussion Space

Discussion should feel like a clean social feed.

Features:

- Book-specific discussion rooms
- Reader comments
- Colored highlights in book club mode
- Private highlights separate from public/book club comments
- Friend invites
- Favorite quote sharing
- Chapter-based discussion threads
- Chat-only version similar to Twitter/X style posting

Post cards should include profile picture, username, post text, story/chapter reference, like/comment/share buttons, and warm gold/plum accent effects.

### Creator Dashboard

Include welcome message, stats cards, uploaded stories, drafts, monetization shortcut, collaboration requests, voice actor invites, badge/tier progress, and analytics charts later.

Stats:

- Reads
- Listens
- Followers
- Revenue
- Comments
- Saves

Creator actions:

- Upload new story
- Add chapter
- Add audio
- Invite narrator
- View earnings
- Edit story
- Manage comments

### Story Upload Flow

Step 1: Add Story Details

- Story title
- Description
- Genre
- Tags
- Age rating
- Language
- Status: ongoing/complete
- Cover upload

Step 2: Upload Content

- Upload text file
- Paste chapter text
- Add chapter manually
- Upload audio file
- Select AI narration fallback
- Invite voice actor

Step 3: Monetization

- Free
- First 5 chapters free
- Ads unlock up to around 10 chapters
- Premium only
- Coin unlock
- Creator subscription exclusive

Step 4: Publish / Preview

- Preview story page
- Save draft
- Publish

### Creator Badges & Tiers

Tier examples:

- Ink Starter
- Story Shaper
- Voice Maven
- Platinum Quill

Badge/tier perks can include more story uploads, deeper analytics, voice upload access, collaboration invites, featured placement, monetization tools, and premium creator badges.

### Monetization Model

AseVerse can make money from subscriptions, ads, sponsorships, events, creator tools, marketplace fees, premium reading/listening, coins/points, exclusive content, and hardback book purchases through the app later.

User-facing model:

- Free tier
- Ad-supported tier
- 7-day free trial
- Premium tiers
- First 5 chapters free
- Up to about 10 chapters through ads
- Coins/points from check-ins or ad views
- Rewards for reading a set number of books per month

### Parental / Age Modes

Teen Mode:

- YA-only access
- No adult content
- Age-appropriate recommendations

Kid Mode:

- Locked mode
- No teen/adult content
- Parent-controlled access

### Profile Page

Include avatar, username, bio, Reader/Creator/Voice Actor badge, saved stories, TBR list, continue reading, achievements, subscription status, coins/points balance, and creator stats if relevant.

### Settings Page

Include account info, theme, font size, font style, notification settings, download/offline settings, parental controls, subscription management, privacy, and logout.

Themes:

- Dark
- Light
- Warm/yellow reading mode

## Technical MVP Priority

Build the MVP in this order:

1. Landing page with waitlist CTA
2. Login/signup
3. Home/discover page
4. Story detail page
5. Reading page
6. Audio player mock
7. Creator dashboard
8. Upload story flow
9. Profile/settings
10. Discussion/book club space

For now, fake/mock backend data. The first demo just needs to look and feel real.

## Demo Goal

The demo should help investors and early users understand that AseVerse is a platform where stories are not just read. They are heard, discussed, monetized, and built collaboratively by authors, readers, and voice actors.
