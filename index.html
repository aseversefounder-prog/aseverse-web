What is AseVerse?

Stories should move between page, voice, and community without losing their soul.

AseVerse blends the discovery of a reading platform, the immersion of an audiobook app, and the tools creators need to publish, collaborate, and grow. It is built for novels, educational stories, history, science, book clubs, and audio-first storytelling.

Core features

One platform for the whole story lifecycle.

Read + Listen

Switch between text, narration, or both with Dual Experience mode.

Creator Tools

Upload stories, manage chapters, track engagement, and prepare monetization.

Voice Collaboration

Connect authors with narrators through profiles, samples, invites, and credits.

Book Clubs

Turn highlights, comments, and chapter reactions into community conversation.

Beyond Novels

Support fiction, romance, history, science, classroom stories, and audio learning.

Premium Discovery

Give readers beautiful story cards, progress, recommendations, and saved libraries.

Who it's for

Built around the people who make stories matter.
