## Visual Direction Observed

- Logo uses gold handwritten lettering over a dark plum/charcoal gradient.
- Primary app mood is premium, warm, dramatic, and audio-forward.
- Core palette in the mockups:
  - Deep plum / wine
  - Near-black charcoal
  - Metallic gold
  - Cream paper
  - Warm sepia/orange story artwork
- Typography mixes a clean sans UI style with large editorial serif story titles and handwritten logo/script accents.
- Buttons often use gold fills or thin gold outlines.
- Navigation icons are simple line icons with gold active states.

## Product Details To Carry Into MVP

- Keep Dual Narration as a highly visible signature feature, not buried in settings.
- Use segmented controls for AI Voice / Human Voice / Dual Narration on audio screens.
- Keep a dedicated StoryMix concept for personalized audio/story playlists.
- Add writing competitions or creator challenges as a later community/creator growth feature.
- Include both dark immersive screens and cream reading/discovery screens.
- Use gold active states consistently for navigation, progress, and primary actions.
- Preserve the handwritten AseVerse logo direction.

## Implementation Preference

For the first demo, build an interactive web/mobile prototype that recreates the strongest mockup patterns:

1. Landing/waitlist using the logo, tagline, and premium dark palette.
2. Discover screen with cream editorial story cards.
3. Dark audio player with the AI/Human/Dual segmented control.
4. Story upload flow using the dark gold-outlined form style.
5. Profile/badges screens using the extracted tier concepts.
